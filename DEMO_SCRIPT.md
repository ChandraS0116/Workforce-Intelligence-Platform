# Workforce Intelligence Platform - Demo Script

**Target Audience**: CTOs, HR Executives, Resource Managers
**Estimated Length**: 5-7 Minutes
**Tone**: Professional, Data-Driven, Visionary

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:45)
**Visual**: Platform Login Screen -> Fade to Dashboard
**Speaking Notes**:
> "Welcome to the Workforce Intelligence Platform—a next-generation decision support system designed to help enterprise IT organizations stop guessing and start predicting."
> "Today, most companies manage their talent in static spreadsheets. This platform turns that raw HR data into actionable, predictive intelligence."
> "Let's log in."

## 2. Executive Dashboard (0:45 - 1:30)
**Visual**: The main `/` Dashboard with utilization charts and KPIs.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "Immediately upon logging in, leadership gets a unified view of organizational health. But these aren't just flat numbers. Our AI engine is constantly aggregating live data from thousands of project assignments, skill matrices, and burnout metrics."
> "You can clearly see a trailing 6-month historical utilization trend. If an area turns red, we know about it before it becomes a delivery failure."

## 3. Executive Command Center (1:30 - 2:30)
**Visual**: Click 'Executive Command Center', then click 'Presentation Mode' to go fullscreen.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "When it's time for the quarterly board meeting, we switch to the Executive Command Center in Presentation Mode. No more building PowerPoint decks."
> "The AI instantly surfaces Strategic Priorities. For example, here it detected a critical skill shortage in Cloud Architecture, calculating the exact business impact and providing a specific hiring recommendation to resolve it in 12 weeks."

## 4. Smart Team Builder (2:30 - 3:30)
**Visual**: Open the `/team-builder` route. Select "React" and "Python".
**Speaking Notes**:
> "How do we actually staff these projects? Traditionally, it's whoever is available. With our Smart Team Builder, you simply input the required skills and project parameters."
> *[Run Builder]*
> "The engine evaluates hundreds of employees instantly—looking at skill matches, current utilization to prevent burnout, and even historical team chemistry—to recommend the statistically perfect squad. It even provides a confidence score for delivery success."

## 5. Team Chemistry & Skill Gaps (3:30 - 4:30)
**Visual**: Navigate to `/chemistry`, hover over nodes. Then go to `/skill-gap`.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "That chemistry score comes from our network analysis engine. We map thousands of historical collaborations to understand which employees work best together and where silos are forming."
> "But we also need to look at the future. The Skill Gap module maps our current talent supply against future project demand. It doesn't just tell us what we lack—it categorizes the gap into 'Buy', 'Build', or 'Borrow' strategies with estimated financial costs."

## 6. Forecasting & Scenario Simulation (4:30 - 5:30)
**Visual**: Open `/forecasting`, then `/scenario-simulator`.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "If we want to peer further into the future, the Forecaster predicts capacity demands up to a year out based on current trends."
> "And if leadership wants to make changes? We use the Scenario Simulator. Let's see what happens if we increase hiring by 15% but face 5% attrition. The engine calculates the exact impact on workforce health and project risk instantly."

## 7. Reporting & Themes (5:30 - 6:30)
**Visual**: `/reporting`. Export a PDF. Then toggle a theme.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "Every piece of data here can be instantly exported into an Executive PDF or Excel sheet for offline distribution."
> "Finally, the platform is designed to feel native to any organization. With a robust design system, we can instantly switch between themes—from Corporate Light to Midnight Gold—ensuring the application always feels premium."

## 8. Conclusion (6:30 - 7:00)
**Visual**: Return to the Dashboard.
**Speaking Notes**:
> "The Workforce Intelligence Platform bridges the gap between HR and Operations. It’s not just about tracking who works here; it’s about knowing how to deploy them to guarantee success."
> "Thank you."
